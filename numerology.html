The basics

What Numerology Is

Numerology is the study of the numbers held in your name and your birth date, and what they reveal about you. Every letter and every date carries a number, and read together they form a kind of map: your character, the path your life is built around, what your heart most wants, and the timing of the seasons you move through. It is old, precise, and surprisingly personal.
